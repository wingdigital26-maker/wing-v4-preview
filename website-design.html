After launch · 04 / 04

What happens after the site launches?

This is where the monthly model earns its keep, and where most websites quietly fall apart. A site that launches and is then abandoned starts aging the same day: prices drift out of date, a service you dropped is still listed, photos go stale, and Google slowly registers that nobody is home.

Maintenance is not an upsell here. It is the point of paying monthly instead of once, and it is why the SEO work and the content can keep building on the site instead of fighting it.

What if I already have a website?

We audit it for free on the consultation and give you the honest read. Sometimes a site needs a full rebuild, sometimes it needs a refresh and some SEO, and when the cheaper path gets you the same result, that is the path we recommend.

Telling an owner their website is fine is a terrible sales strategy, and we do it anyway.

The owners who really do need a rebuild tend to remember the company that was straight with them. Bigger builds, the ones with a page for every city you serve or extras like galleries and booking, get a clear timeline and an exact quote up front on the free consultation instead of an optimistic guess. Either way you know the launch date before we start, and we hit it.

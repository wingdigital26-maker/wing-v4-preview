Denton, TX · Top of the metroplex

Denton is not a Dallas suburb, so stop being sold to like one.

A courthouse square, two universities and an economy that runs on its own calendar. Most agencies down the tollway treat all of that as a rounding error.

Its own economy · 01 / 04

A city with a term calendar does not behave like one with a commute.

Denton sits at the top of the metroplex where I-35 splits, far enough north that a Dallas agency writes it into a service area and then never thinks about it again. But it is a county seat with a working courthouse square, two universities in the middle of town, and a music and food scene that pulls people up from cities that have plenty of both already.

That produces a business rhythm nothing in Plano or Irving shares. August is a different month here. So is May. A lot of demand arrives and leaves on a term schedule, and it arrives twice over, because the crowd around the square on a Saturday night and the households out toward the newer development on the west side are not the same customers and do not search the same way.

An agency that has never noticed any of that will still sell you a page with the word Denton on it. It will just be a page written for a suburb, aimed at a city that is not one.

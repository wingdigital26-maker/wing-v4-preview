Referrals run out · 01 / 04

A reputation that only travels by mouth has a range limit.

Garland is old enough and big enough to have raised its own trades. Plenty of the electricians, fabricators, plumbers, landscapers and shop owners here have run full books for twenty years without a website worth the name, because the phone rang anyway. That works right up until the people who knew you retire, move, or hand the decision to a son-in-law who opens Google instead.

Then the reputation stops travelling. What a stranger finds is a Google listing with the wrong hours, four reviews from 2019 and no site behind it. Meanwhile a newer outfit with half your skill and a real page takes the call, because it is the only thing that answered the question.

None of that is a Garland problem specifically. It just bites harder here than it does in a suburb that was built and marketed in the same decade. The fix is not a rebrand. It is putting the twenty years you already have somewhere a search engine can read it.

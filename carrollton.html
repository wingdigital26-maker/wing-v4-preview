Carrollton, TX · Three counties

Carrollton is three cities wearing one name.

A city whose limits cross Dallas, Denton and Collin counties does not have one local search result. It has several, and most sites are set up to win only one.

The county problem · 01 / 04

Your address settles an argument you were never part of.

Two shops on the same Carrollton road can file under different counties, land in different sets of local results, and be described by their own customers with different words. One calls itself north Dallas. One calls itself Denton County. Neither is wrong, and a site that only ever writes the word Carrollton has quietly opted out of both conversations.

The same split runs through the neighbourhoods. Trinity Mills and Josey Lane do not feel like the restored downtown square beside the rail station, and neither of those feels like the run of Korean and Latino owned businesses along Old Denton Road. Carrollton was built out long before the newer suburbs north of it, so the housing, the shopfronts and the customers are all older and more mixed than a map suggests. People near one pocket call you for different reasons than people near another, and they type those reasons into the search box.

The work here is not writing the city name more often. It is deciding which of those pockets earns a real page with real reasons on it, and letting one honest citywide page carry the rest.

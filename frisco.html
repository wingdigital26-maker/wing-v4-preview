The Frisco problem · 01 / 04

A city this new has no old reputation to inherit.

In a town that has been settled for a century, a trade wins work because somebody's father used them. Frisco does not run on that. Whole neighbourhoods off Eldorado Parkway and out past Panther Creek went up inside a decade, filled with people who moved here from somewhere else, and the person who just bought the house has nobody local to ask. So they search. That is the whole referral network, compressed into one results page.

The second half of the problem is that the search does not stop at the city line. A homeowner near the Star or off Warren Parkway typing your trade plus Frisco gets served companies operating out of Plano, McKinney, Little Elm and The Colony, all of whom will happily drive fifteen minutes. You are not competing with the businesses on your street. You are competing with everyone inside a twenty minute radius who took their website seriously.

None of that is fixed by adding the word Frisco to your homepage. It is fixed by being the result that answers the question, loads fast on a phone in a driveway, and has enough recent proof attached that a stranger will call you instead of the next one down.
